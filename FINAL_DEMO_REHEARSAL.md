# RizIntel — Final Evaluator Demo Rehearsal & Presentation Script

This document details the exact 5–7 minute live demonstration sequence for presenting RizIntel to evaluators at the Cognizant NPN Cybersecurity Hackathon.

---

## Rehearsal Sequencing Table (Total Target Duration: 6 Minutes 15 Seconds)

| Step | Segment / Goal | UI Page | Action Performed | What Evaluator Sees | Presenter Script ("What to Say") | Backend Empirical Proof | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Problem & Introduction** | Login Portal (`/login`) | Present problem statement & log in as `lead@rizintel.demo`. | Login form with secure JWT credentials input. | *"AppSec teams face massive alert fatigue from multi-scanner noise, with up to 70% duplicated findings and uncontextualized CVSS scores. RizIntel is a Security Decision Intelligence Platform that unifies multi-scanner ingestion, correlates duplicates, filters noise, enriches threat intel, and visualizes 8-stage decision provenance."* | `auth.py` -> `login_for_access_token()` | 30s |
| **2** | **Workspace & Org Scope** | Workspace (`/workspace`) | Inspect organization workspace dashboard. | Workspace metrics, total assets, scan runs summary, active org `ORG-DEMO-001`. | *"Here in the Organization Workspace, all metrics and scan data are strictly isolated by tenant organization ID `ORG-DEMO-001`. | **3** | **Authorized Target Asset** | Asset Registry (`/asset-registry`) | View registered target asset `ASSET-WEBGOAT-001`. | Asset `ASSET-WEBGOAT-001`, host `127.0.0.1:8085`, environment `LAB`, `AUTHORIZATION: AUTHORIZED`. | *"Before any scanner can execute, the target asset must be registered and explicitly marked AUTHORIZED by a Security Lead. | **4** | **Active Scanner Agent** | Scanner Agents (`/scanner-agents`) | View registered machine agent `AGENT-6ED5110074`. | Agent `AGENT-6ED5110074`, status `ACTIVE`, capability `NUCLEI = AVAILABLE`. | *"Here is our active machine scanner agent. | **9** | **Real-Time SSE Updates** | Live Scan Visualizer (`/scan-runs`) | Observe real-time SSE stream events broadcasting in UI. | Live event feed rendering stage events (`M1_NORMALIZE` through `M7_SLA`). | *"RizIntel streams pipeline stage events in real time using single-use, short-lived SSE tickets. | **10** | **M1$\to$M7 Pipeline Progression** | Live Pipeline Stepper (`/scan-runs`) | Watch pipeline stepper advance from M1 to M7. | Visual stepper nodes lighting up: M1 Normalization $\to$ M2 Deduplication $\to$ M3 Confidence $\to$ M4 Threat Intel $\to$ M5 Risk $\to$ M6 Explain $\to$ M7 SLA. | *"Our M1 through M7 pipeline normalizes findings into Schema v1.0, correlates duplicates across scanners, filters noise, enriches threat intel via EPSS and CISA KEV, calculates context-aware risk scores, and assigns SLA deadlines."* | `services/pipeline_service.py` -> `execute_pipeline()` | 30s |
| **11** | **Live Metrics & Counts** | Live Counts Grid (`/scan-runs`) | Inspect live raw vs canonical counts. | Raw Findings: 1, Canonical: 1, Actionable: 1, Pipeline Duration: ~1.2s. | *"Live counts show exact pipeline transformation metrics: raw findings ingested vs actionable findings prioritized."* | `services/pipeline_service.py` summary metrics dict | 15s |

| **14** | **Open Finding360** | Finding360 Modal (`/findings/FIN-2026-F61FAEA95204`) | Click **Finding 360** view button. | Modal displaying 360-degree tabs: Overview, Threat Intel, Asset Context, Risk Breakdown, Explanation, SLA. | *"Finding360 consolidates every dimension of the vulnerability into a single pane: 5-signal confidence classification, asset criticality, risk driver breakdown, and AI explainability."* | `routers/findings.py` -> `get_finding_details()` | 30s |
| **15** | **RizTrace Decision Provenance** | RizTrace Graph (`/provenance?scan_run_id=...`) | Click **RizTrace Decision Provenance** tab. | Interactive 8-stage decision lineage graph from Raw Signal $\to$ M1 $\to$ M2 $\to$ M3 $\to$ M4 $\to$ M5 $\to$ M6 $\to$ M7 $\to$ Decision. | *"This is RizTrace—our decision provenance graph.
